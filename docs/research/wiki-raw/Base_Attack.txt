This page lists items that grant a **Base Attack** value. Base Attack is the flat attack shown in an item's stats; temporary buffs or conditional gains are not part of base stats.
Related effects that set, scale, or convert your attack include:
Prime Form — double attack while health is full
Battle Axe — while the enemy has armor, double your attack
Granite Hammer — on hit: convert armor to attack
Wave Breaker — scales with negative base attack
Rusty Sword — First Turn: Give acid equal to your attack on hit
