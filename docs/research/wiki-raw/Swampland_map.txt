
An island
The following locations can be found anywhere on the map:
- [Chest.png] Treasure Chests allow choosing between one of three Common rarity items to add to the inventory. There is a 1/80 chance items will be Golden and a 1/500 chance the items will be Diamond. More common in the Starting Forest biome.
- [Event ferryman.png] Ferryman takes you to one of three other islands. Each island will have one, which is always visible when the player is near.
- [Bazaar vendor.png] Bargaining Tents have a selection of 2 Rare rarity items that can be bought for 5 Gold. Can choose to haggle, which has a 75% chance to change the price to 4 and a 25% chance to change the price to 7. Cannot appear on the Starting Island.
- [Bladeoil.png] Blade Oils allow choosing between giving the weapon 1 Attack, Armor or Speed. Refreshes if the weapon is changed. Each oil can only be applied once per weapon.
- [Campfire.png] Campfires can be used at night to wait until the next day and restore 10 Health.
- [Blacksmith.png] Forges allow choosing between two bonuses to add to the weapon. If weapon already has one it can be replaced for 10 Gold.
- [Sinkhole.png] Sinkhole takes you to a random island. Cannot appear on the Starting Island.
