History
Restore health equal to regeneration at turn end, then remove 1 stack.
Mechanics
At the end of your turn, you restore health equal to your current Regeneration stacks, then 1 stack is removed.
If multiple sources grant Regeneration during the same turn, they add together before the end-of-turn heal.
Regeneration is part of the Status effect tags.
