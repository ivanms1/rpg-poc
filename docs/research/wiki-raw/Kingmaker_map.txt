
The following locations can be found anywhere on the map:
- [Chest.png] Treasure Chests allow choosing between one of three Common rarity items to add to the inventory. There is a 1/80 chance items will be Golden and a 1/500 chance the items will be Diamond. More common in the Starting Area biome.
- [Bazaar vendor.png] Bargaining Tents have a selection of 2 Rare rarity items that can be bought for 5 Gold. Can choose to haggle, which has a 75% chance to change the price to 4 and a 25% chance to change the price to 7. Cannot appear on the Starting Island.
- [Bladeoil.png] Blade Oils allow choosing between giving the weapon 1 Attack, Armor or Speed. Refreshes if the weapon is changed. Each oil can only be applied once per weapon.
- [Campfire.png] Campfires can be used at night to wait until the next day and restore 10 Health.
- [Tower.png] Lookout Towers reveal the map in a 13 tile radius around them.
- [Woodcutter.png] Woodcutters can combine two items into a random item of Heroic rarity.
- [Summoning circle.png] Scrying Pools reveal a new rare location on the map.
- [Waypoint.png] Waypoints allow traveling to them instantly at any point by hovering over them in map mode and pressing Space. They are always visible when the player is near.
- [Donkey carriage.png] Horse Carriages allow traveling to one of three biomes and reveal all tiles along the way.
The following locations can be found anywhere on the map but are very rare:
- [Beehive.png] Beehive: gives a Honeycomb that can be combined with food items at a Cauldron but is protected by Honey Bee enemies.
- [Crone.png] Crone: choose between two cursed items.
- [Junksmith.png] Junksmith: can choose to copy an item.
- [Big golem.png] Large Golem: upgrade a regular item into a Golden one or a Golden item into a Diamond one.
- [Rune carver.png] Rune Stone: can choose between the following items: Blood Rune, Echo Rune, Iron Rune.
- [Gem expert.png] Gem Stash: can choose between the following items: Ruby Gemstone, Sapphire Gemstone, and Citrine Gemstone. (Need Woodland Blue Bundle)
- [Fairy queen.png] Fairy Queen: can transform all items into other items of higher rarity
- [Alchemist.png] Alchemist: Upgrades a selected potion into an elixir.
- [Shady dealer.png] Rich Merchant: has a selection of 6 Heroic rarity items that can be bought with 5 gold.
- [Beekeeper.png] Beekeeper: sells 2 honey items for 25 gold each.
