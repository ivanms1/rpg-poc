Challenges are conditions that unlock new items for all runs once completed. Challenges are displayed in the Compendium, where they are arranged in a 13x5 grid. Their conditions and bonuses are hidden until completed but can be revealed by completing two adjacent challenges. Both maps have 65 challenges each. The following challenges exist for Swampland:
