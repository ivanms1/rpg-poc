## Swampland Hydra
The final fight on the Swampland maps is against 3 out of 4 Swampland Hydra heads, in a predefined order. Before the fight the player will be shown all four heads and asked to remove one of them. The player will not recover health or lose status effects between the fights. The next Hydra head will keep the previous one's status effects also.
