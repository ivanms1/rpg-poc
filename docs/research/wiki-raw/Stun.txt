Mechanics
Stun prevents your strike on that turn.
After the skipped strike, remove 1 Stun stack. Multiple stacks will cause multiple skipped strikes across turns.
Non-strike item effects (e.g., Battle Start, Turn Start, Countdown) can still trigger while stunned.
Stun is part of the Status effect tags.
