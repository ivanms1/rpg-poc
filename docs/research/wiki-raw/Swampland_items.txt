
Swampland items can only be found in Swampland or Kingmaker games that use Swampland bundles. Swampland items come in 5 levels of rarity: Common, Rare, Heroic, Mythic, and Cursed. Items with Cursed rarity can only be obtained from a Crone.
Swampland items may inflict the following status effects on either enemies or the player:
- [Icon acid.png] Acid removes armor equal to acid at the start of the turn
- [Icon poison.png] Poison deals damage equal to the amount of poison at turn start if you have no armor and removes 1 poison stack at the start of the turn
- [Icon regen.png] Regeneration restores health equal to the amount of regeneration at turn end and removes 1 regeneration at the end of the turn
- [Icon riptide.png] Riptide deals 5 damage at the end of the turn and removes 1 riptide at the end of the turn
- [Icon stun.png] Stun skips the attack and removes 1 stun at the end of the turn
- [Icon purity.png] Purity gives 1 attack per stack and restores 3 health when removed
