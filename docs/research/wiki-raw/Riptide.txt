Mechanics
At the end of the afflicted unit’s turn, Riptide triggers once: they take 5 damage and remove 1 Riptide stack.
With multiple stacks, Riptide will keep triggering on consecutive turns.
Effects like Stillwater Pearl can change the number of triggers per turn.
Riptide is part of the Status effect tags.
