Mechanics
When the player is struck, damage is dealt equal to the current number of Thorns stacks.
Thorns are only removed at the end of the turn if they have been triggered.
If the enemy is stunned or otherwise skips their strike, Thorns remain into the next round.
Thorns is part of the Status effect tags.
